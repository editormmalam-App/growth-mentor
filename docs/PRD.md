# Growth Mentor — PRD

## Problem
Productivity tools optimize for marginal, linear improvement — keeping users stuck in safe cycles. Growth Mentor flips that: a 10-year vision is the top constraint, and every goal, habit, and weekly scorecard is measured against exponential (10x) potential, not 2x survival.

## Target User
The builder and their students — people who want the effect of an expensive personal growth coach without the human cost. Self-directed, motivated, willing to log weekly and self-correct.

## Core Objects
- **Vision** — a single 10-year north-star statement; the system's top constraint.
- **Goal** — short-term (≤3 mo) or long-term (3 mo–10 yr), linked to a Vision, tagged by pillar (health, soft-skills, education, career, finance).
- **Weekly Scorecard** — one per ISO week; aggregates scores across all active goals.
- **Scorecard Entry** — per-goal weekly self-score (1–10) + notes on effort, progress, blockers.
- **Habit** — repeating action tied to a goal (e.g. "read 20 min", "gym 3x/wk").

## MVP (v1) — must-haves
- [ ] Create/edit one Vision statement.
- [ ] Create/edit goals (short/long-term, pillar-tagged, linked to vision).
- [ ] Generate a weekly scorecard; score each active goal 1–10 with notes.
- [ ] See weekly trend (score over recent weeks) and pillar breakdown.
- [ ] Mark goals complete / archive.

## Non-goals (v1)
- No human-in-the-loop coaching, AI advice, or mentor matching.
- No social/sharing, no payments, no notifications.
- No auth wall — demo-first, viewable by anonymous visitors.

## Success criteria
**A user creates a vision, adds 3 goals across different pillars, opens this week's scorecard, scores all 3 goals, saves, and sees their trend chart update — all without logging in.**
